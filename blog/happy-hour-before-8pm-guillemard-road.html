Happy Hour Before 8pm on Guillemard Road

Happy hour at Hippies Bar & Grill is every day before 8:00pm. The kitchen and bar run the same hours, 4:30pm to midnight, and the price on the menu is the price on the bill.

The window is the early evening

Doors open at 4:30pm. Happy hour runs until 8:00pm, seven days a week. That is the stretch for an after-work pint before the room fills, not a one-day weekday special. The site does not publish a separate discount table here. Current happy-hour pricing is on the full menu. What this page can tell you is the timing, and the nett prices already printed for the taps and the kitchen.

What a nett pint means

No GST and no service charge are added on top. Draught prices already listed, half pint then full pint, are Stella Artois at $10 / $12, Heineken at $11 / $13, Guinness Draught at $12 / $15, and Little Creatures Pale Ale at $12 / $15. Goose Island IPA is on tap, and its current price is on the full menu. Corona Extra is $14 a bottle, or $45 for a bucket of five.

Eat if you are staying

The kitchen is open for the same hours as the bar. The ribeye is $19.90 nett. Tobiko pasta is $19.90. Beef chilli with nachos is $15.90, nachos with salsa are $14, and buffalo mid-wings are $14. The Hippies Bites Platter is $35. None of those figures grows by a service charge at the end of the night.

Getting a seat

Walk-ins are welcome. Groups, weekends, and steak nights should reserve on WhatsApp or by phone at +65 8881 8391. There is indoor air-conditioning and a semi-outdoor area, with rock music at night. Street parking is along Guillemard Road and the nearby streets, and it fills up. The bar is at 391 Guillemard Road, Singapore 399789, near Geylang, Kallang, and Paya Lebar.
