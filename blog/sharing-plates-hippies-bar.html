Sharing plates at Hippies: the nett platter, wings, and nachos

A table for two or four can start with the Hippies Bites Platter at $35 nett, then add the $14 plates. The menu price is the price. There is no GST and no service charge.

The platter

Hippies' Bites Platter is $35 nett. The menu describes popcorn chicken, curly fries, shoestring fries, crispy chicken wings, chicken nuggets, spam fries, and sotong balls. It is marked spicy. It is built for the middle of the table, not as a single main.

The $14 plates

Nachos are $14 nett: tortilla chips with house-made salsa, marked with a leaf on the menu (meat-free as listed). Truffle fries are $14 nett, shoestring fries in truffle-infused olive oil and parmesan, also marked with a leaf. Buffalo mid-wings are $14 nett for 6 pieces in a spicy, tangy sauce. Togarashi mid-wings are $14 nett for 6 pieces. Spicy roasted chicken drumlets are listed at a promo price of $11.90, usual price $14, for 6 pieces.

How to order them

Open daily from 4:30pm to midnight at 391 Guillemard Road, a 5-minute walk from Dakota MRT. Call or WhatsApp +65 8881 8391 if you want a table. The ribeye, at $19.90 nett, is a separate plate and has its own guide. Draught prices are on the tap list, not bundled into these plates.
